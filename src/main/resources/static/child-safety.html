Child Safety Standards

Pride Universal (Android: com.prideuniversal.app) · Published by Code Implants · Effective 20 November 2026

Pride Universal is the private society portal of the Pride Universal co-operative housing society. Only residents, committee members and staff registered by the society office can sign in. This page sets out our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), in line with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

Zero tolerance

Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any content or behaviour that sexualises, grooms, exploits or endangers children are strictly prohibited in every part of the app, including notices, complaints, marketplace and business listings, vendor entries and any other content members can post.

How to report

Anyone can report suspected CSAE content or behaviour connected with the app by emailing hr@codeimplants.com, or by telling the society's managing committee. Please include where in the app it appeared and, if possible, the date and the flat or person involved. Do not forward or save the material itself.

What we do

  • We review every report promptly and remove violating content as soon as it is confirmed.
  • Accounts involved are suspended and permanently removed from the app.
  • We report CSAM and child-exploitation offences to the appropriate authorities, including the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and the police, as required by Indian law, including the POCSO Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, and we preserve relevant records for investigators.
  • The committee can remove content and deactivate accounts from within the app at any time.

Contact

Child safety point of contact: hr@codeimplants.com (Code Implants, developer of Pride Universal). This contact can speak to our CSAM prevention practices and compliance.
